# Third-party provenance and distribution scope

The method is from SuperMimic (Liao et al., 2026): https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28480 .

Experiments used Shimingyi/allmimic at commit `cb334ff674b2f57248f33f8c21b7fb526fbdf8cc`: https://github.com/Shimingyi/allmimic/tree/cb334ff674b2f57248f33f8c21b7fb526fbdf8cc . Its LICENSE at this commit is a placeholder and explicitly notes unresolved redistribution rights for code derived from dhmbb2/supermimic_vibe. We therefore link to, rather than vendor, the training framework.

This repository provides independently authored experiment wrappers, validators, documentation, and generated results. The robot model, original motion files, upstream implementation, and trained checkpoints are not bundled. Motion provenance is lvhaidong/LAFAN1_Retargeting_Dataset, revision `ce1572906efe6157840e8474d5a0d7aa87481e74`; obtain data from its source under applicable terms. Rendered clips are experiment demonstrations, not a redistribution of the motion library or robot assets.

No general open-source license is selected for this repository's independent artifacts yet. Do not interpret the upstream placeholder as an MIT/BSD/Apache license or infer redistribution permission for upstream code, data, or assets.
